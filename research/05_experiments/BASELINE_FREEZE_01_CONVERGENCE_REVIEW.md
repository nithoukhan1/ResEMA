## BASE-B-ORG-SCR-S42 budget decision

The frozen protocol states:

> If the best epoch is near the boundary and validation mAP50-95 is still materially
> rising, register an explicit longer-budget experiment rather than silently extending
> the completed run.

The full trajectory satisfies the trigger strongly enough to register a diagnostic:

- best epoch = 100;
- last-5 slope = +0.000862 mAP50-95 / epoch;
- last-10 slope = +0.0004823 / epoch;
- last-20 slope = +0.00034785 / epoch;
- last-30 slope = +0.00020885 / epoch;
- last-50 slope = +0.00022559 / epoch;
- final epoch exceeds epoch 99 by +0.00361;
- rolling 5-, 10-, and 20-epoch mean maxima occur at the end of training.

The signal is not treated as proof that 200 epochs will improve the selected result.
Validation localization losses do not provide a uniformly improving story, and the
epoch-100 point is unusually high relative to the immediately preceding values.
Therefore the correct action is an **explicit new longer-budget diagnostic**, not a
reinterpretation or continuation of the frozen 100-epoch baseline.

The completed 100-epoch run remains canonical and immutable.

Proposed future diagnostic ID:

`BASE-B-ORG-SCR-S42-E200-CAL`

The experiment must be registered separately under a new governed source/authorization
transaction. It must not overwrite or resume the finalized 100-epoch run.
